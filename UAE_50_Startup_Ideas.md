## 3. ALL 55 IDEAS

### A. Fintech & wealth (GetGold-style)
1. **Jamiya / Committee app** — Digital rotating savings circles with a guaranteed payout order and default protection. Payments are reported to AECB so they build credit.
   - Proven: MoneyFellows (Egypt, ~$31M Series B ⚠️), Hakbah (KSA, SAMA-approved ✅), Wafir (Qatar ✅).
   - UAE: only "Roundz", very early ✅.
   - Revenue: fee for early-payout slots plus float income.
   - Licence: CBUAE sandbox. **D5 E3 M4**
2. **Credit Builder** — A secured card or micro-loan that reports to AECB from day 1, plus import of the user's home-country credit history.
   - Proven: Self, Chime, Kikoff (US) ⚠️.
   - UAE: only bank fixed-deposit-backed cards ✅.
   - Revenue: AED 15–30/month subscription plus lender referrals. **D5 E3 M4**
3. **Save-While-You-Send** — Each remittance automatically splits off a slice into a goal: gold, a home-country deposit, school fees back home, a land fund.
   - Proven: Beam&Go (Philippines) ✅.
   - Partner with an exchange house. **D4 E3 M3**
4. **Gratuity (EOSB) Tracker & Investor** — Shows accrued gratuity live, lets users make voluntary top-ups into sukuk or gold, and offers advances against vested gratuity.
   - Analogue: PensionBee (UK) ⚠️.
   - UAE: only DEWS (DIFC and government staff) ✅. **D4 E3 M4**
5. **Round-up Halal Investing** — Rounds up card spending into gold, sukuk or a halal ETF.
   - Proven: Acorns, Raiz ⚠️.
   - UAE: none found ✅. **D3 E3 M2**
6. **Gold SIP / Daily Gold** — A Jar-style automatic daily AED 5 gold purchase with a gamified streak.
   - Jar: 35M users ✅.
   - The UAE has gold apps but no daily-SIP product. Low cost to build as a feature or white-label for banks. **D4 E4 M2**
7. **Gold-Backed Salary Advance** — An advance secured against the user's own fractional gold, so it is cheaper than fee-based advances.
   - New idea. **D4 E2 M4**
8. **Blue-collar Sharia micro-investing** — Starts from AED 10, in Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam and Tagalog.
   - Proven: Bibit (Indonesia, ~5M+ users ⚠️).
   - Wahed and Sarwa target higher-income users. **D4 E2 M3**
9. **Payroll-card micro-insurance** — AED 10/month for repatriation of remains, family health cover back home, and life cover.
   - Proven: BIMA, M-Tiba ⚠️.
   - Needs a CBUAE broker licence. **D4 E3 M3**
10. **Save-First Layaway** — Save toward an iPhone or a flight ticket, and the merchant adds a bonus.
    - Proven: Cushion, Plaza ⚠️.
    - Fits the CBUAE's 2024 BNPL rules. **D3 E4 M2**
11. **Family Remote Allowance Card** — A UAE-based parent funds and controls a card used by a spouse or child in India, Pakistan or the Philippines.
    - New idea: remittance plus Greenlight-style controls. **D4 E2 M4**
12. **Visa-Cycle Wallet ("Going-Home Fund")** — Knows the user's contract end date and auto-saves toward it. Pays gratuity plus savings out cross-border at exit.
    - New idea. **D4 E2 M4**
13. **Alternative Credit File** — Packages ROSCA, rent and remittance payment history into a credit report that banks can buy.
    - Proven: Esusu (US, $1bn valuation) ⚠️. **D4 E2 M5**
14. **Employer-Matched Savings for WPS workers** — A Gulf "401(k) match" delivered through payroll cards.
    - New idea. Employers get an ESG and welfare badge. **D3 E2 M4**
15. **Rent-payment credit reporting** — Reports on-time rent to AECB.
    - Proven: Experian RentBureau ⚠️.
    - Partner with Keyper or Ejari. **D3 E3 M4**
16. **Rent deposit replacement** — Tenants pay a fee of about one week's rent instead of a 5% deposit.
    - Proven: Zero Deposit (UK), Rhino (US) ⚠️.
    - UAE: none found. **D4 E3 M3**
17. **Wedding & Gold Savings Plan** — Families save monthly toward wedding jewellery and lock the gold price in instalments. Jewellers add a bonus.
    - India's Tanishq "Golden Harvest"-style schemes prove it ⚠️. Digital version: new. **D4 E4 M3**
18. **Hajj/Umrah Savings Plan** — A Sharia goal-savings plan linked to licensed Hajj/Umrah operators.
    - Proven: Tabung Haji (Malaysia) ⚠️. **D4 E3 M3**
19. **Fractional Off-Plan Property Notes** — From AED 2,000, backed by escrow-verified progress (see #30).
    - Stake and SmartCrowd exist but focus on ready properties ⚠️. **D3 E2 M3**
20. **Car-ownership "Total Cost" wallet** — Puts salik (road tolls), fines, insurance, service and registration in one prepaid pot with auto-pay and savings.
    - New idea. **D3 E4 M2**

### B. SME / B2B / Property

    - Proven: Vyapar/ClearTax (India), Qoyod/Wafeq (KSA, after ZATCA e-invoicing) ⚠️.
    - Hard deadline in 2027 ✅. **D5 E3 M3**

    - Proven: Konfío (Mexico) ⚠️. **D4 E2 M5**
23. **Free Zone Qualifying-Income Monitor** — Classifies transactions to protect the 0% free-zone tax rate.
