One operating picture, built around people

Construction teams can move from tender and mobilisation to handover without losing the commercial story. Facilities teams can connect locations, assets, planned maintenance, service levels and field evidence.

Majal is deliberately practical: a clear home screen for each role, focused forms for the field, and linked records that make the next action obvious.

Trust and control are part of the product

Every action is tied to a role, a project, a building or an asset. Approval routes, separation of duties, audit trails, secure asset tags and controlled sharing help organisations keep sensitive commercial and operational records in the right hands.

Majal supports English and Arabic interfaces, responsive field use, offline-safe drafts and guided recovery processes. Security configuration, hosting controls and data-retention choices are agreed during implementation.

Delivered the way your organisation needs

Customers can use Majal as a managed hosted workspace or choose a private deployment with an agreed operating model. We handle environment setup, data migration, training, integrations and release planning with your team.

A clear implementation boundary

Customer-facing product pages focus on business outcomes. Detailed implementation and integration material is shared with approved customers and partners under the relevant agreement.

For customers and delivery partners

We provide role guides, API documentation, integration patterns, data dictionaries and operational runbooks as part of an approved engagement. Source and build access, where appropriate to the contracted solution and applicable notices, is handled privately through the customer success process.
