## Notes on tone
============================================================================
- Keep it warm but concise — 250-350 words total is the sweet spot.
- Never repeat the resume verbatim; the cover letter's job is to add the
  "why this company, why now" that a resume can't carry.
- If a job ad explicitly asks a screening question (e.g., "why do you want
  to work here," "describe a time you retained an at-risk account"), answer
  it directly in Paragraph 1 or 3 rather than forcing it into the bullets.
