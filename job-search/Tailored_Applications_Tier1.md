Ready-to-submit materials for every Tier-1 job in the tracker: which resume to use, which bullets to feature, a full cover letter, and screening-question answers (salary AED 20,000/month, 30-day notice, UAE Employment Visa confirmed).

## How to use this per job

1. Find the job below (use the index or your browser's find-in-page).
2. Open the resume PDF named in the **Resume Variant** column — it's already built; just confirm the 3-4 bullets listed match what's in that PDF (they will, since the variants were built from the same bullet bank).
3. Copy the cover letter text, replace `[Date]`, and export/paste per the platform's application flow.
4. Use the screening-question answers as-is, or adjust if a posting asks something different.
5. Where a note flags something (experience-gap, Saudi work-authorization, unverified URL, possible duplicate employer) — read it before applying. These aren't filler; they're real judgment calls worth 10 seconds of your attention.

## Two resume variants used throughout

- **CSM** = `Mohamed_Amer_CV_ATS_CSM.pdf` — retention/onboarding/adoption/QBR framing. Used for 24 jobs.
- **AM** = `Mohamed_Amer_CV_ATS_AM.pdf` — account growth/expansion/relationship framing. Used for 19 jobs.

Both live in `cv/`. The bullets listed per job below are drawn from these two variants — no new resume files were created per job; swap the headline/bullet emphasis by hand only if you want to go further than the variant already provides.

## Honest notes on this batch

- A few jobs ask for more years of experience than the candidate has (Botpress 7-10, Alaan Enterprise 6-11, Dataiku 7-12, IDG 8-10) — the cover letters address this directly rather than hiding it, leading with results and trajectory instead of tenure. Worth a quick read before applying to these four.
- Saudi Arabia-based roles: the candidate holds a **UAE** Employment Visa only, not Saudi work authorization. Cover letters for KSA roles say this plainly and note relocation would require employer-sponsored KSA sponsorship — don't imply otherwise in an interview.
- A couple of postings (Swvl, Oracle, Adobe) could not be independently confirmed as currently live — each is flagged with a note on what to verify before investing time.
- Two employers appear twice under genuinely different roles (Alaan: Mid-Market vs. Enterprise CSM; Bayut: Senior Sales AM vs. Motors/Partnerships AM) — these are real, separate openings, not duplicates.
