# Get Gold print kit

Five brochures for different audiences and two A–Z guides, each in English (`-en`) and Arabic (`-ar`), as print-ready PDFs. Cover previews are in `covers/`.

| File | Audience | Format | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| getgold-brochure-1-buyers | Gold buyers | A4 landscape tri-fold (folds to 99 × 210 mm) | 2 |
| getgold-brochure-2-wedding | Couples and families | A5 booklet | 8 |
| getgold-brochure-3-bars-coins | Bullion buyers | DL flyer, 99 × 210 mm, double-sided | 2 |
| getgold-brochure-4-jewellers | Jewellers (founding stores) | A4, 4 pages | 4 |
| getgold-brochure-5-investors | Investors and partners | A4 landscape brief | 6 |
| getgold-a-z-buying-gold-booklet | Gold buyers | A5 booklet | 16 |
| getgold-a-z-buying-gold-pocket | Gold buyers | A4, one page | 1 |
| getgold-a-z-selling-booklet | Jewellers | A5 booklet | 16 |
| getgold-a-z-selling-pocket | Jewellers | A4, one page | 1 |

The English A–Z guides run A to Z. The Arabic editions are ordered by the Arabic alphabet (أ to ي).

Printing: the PDFs have no bleed and are in reading order (the printer arranges booklets for folding). Print at 100%: the diamond circles in the wedding booklet are drawn at real size. QR codes open getgold.ae (or /ar for Arabic) and are tagged `utm_source=print&utm_medium=brochure&utm_campaign=<piece>`.

Facts and wording come from getgold.ae on 22 Oct 2026. Worked prices use the 24K reference of AED 482.65/g on that date and are labelled as examples. Market figures are sourced on the pages (World Gold Council, Khaleej Times, Emirates 24|7, Aletihad, World Bank Pink Sheet, DMCC). The jeweller pieces state that the signed Vendor Pre-Agreement is the binding document.
