# Brag Plan: Get Gold (getgold.ae)

## What is this app?
Get Gold is a bilingual (English and Arabic) marketplace of verified UAE jewellers. It shows the live gold price, compares stores by final total or making charge, sizes your ring on screen, and lets you send one request to many stores. Its own line is "Bringing the UAE gold market online."

## The angle
A feature-launch film in the style of Odoo's "Meet Odoo 20's best features" (reference: youtube.com/watch?v=YlmN5QcAdLs), translated into Get Gold's jade and gold. The reference has a clear grammar:
- a cosmic intro with the app icons in orbit;
- white chapter cards with a flat icon and a line-art doodle flying past;
- real UI floating in tilted 3D windows over a soft gradient;
- yellow handwritten stickers;
- magnifier bubbles on the important rows;
- a simulated cursor and typing;
- whip pans between chapters;
- an end card with a handwritten tagline.

We keep that grammar and swap every noun. The purple sky becomes a jade night with gold dust. The app icons become jewellery and Get Gold's features. The rocket doodle becomes a falcon and a dhow. The yellow stickers keep their yellow, because Get Gold's gold (#E9BD50) already is that yellow. The screens are the real Get Gold UI, rebuilt from the app source with real labels and real numbers from the app's own pricing formula.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A ring of 16 flat gold icons orbits in a tilted 3D plane on a deep jade starfield: ring, bangle, chain, coin, bar, diamond, scales, shield, bell, ruler, pin, store and more. A handwritten "Meet" writes itself on. At 1.6 s, on the track's first strong beat, the orbit collapses into the centre. A spinning 3D gold ring and the GET GOLD wordmark slam in with the UAE flag stripe and a yellow starburst. Then "Bringing the UAE gold market online." holds for almost two seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **GetGold Compare.** Four store cards land on the beat inside a tilted browser window: 22K, 10 g, AED 444.04/g, each with an "Estimated final total" and a "Get Gold Value Score".
  - The cursor clicks "Lowest final total" and the cards re-rank: Store C moves to first place with the gold ring and the "Lowest final total" badge.
  - A magnifier bubble slides over its breakdown: Gold value AED 4,440.38 · Making after discount AED 290.00 · Get Gold fee AED 47.30 · VAT (5%) AED 238.88.
  - A yellow sticker reads "Every charge explained".
- **Size guide.** The 3D gold band sits on the screen and the sizing circle grows to meet it.
  - The size counts up to 54, "International size (EU / ISO)", with US 7 · Indian 14 · Diameter 17.3 mm.
  - The cursor clicks "Save as my size (EU 54)" and it turns jade with a tick.
  - Sticker: "Match a ring on screen".
- **Get Gold Request.** "Can't find it? Let stores find it for you."
  - Chips select "bangle", "22 K" and "Dubai", then the description types itself with key ticks.
  - The cursor clicks "Send request to verified stores".
  - Three item-specific offers slide in, each showing making and certificate fees.
  - Sticker: "Send one request".
- **Everything else, in one breath.** This is the Odoo app-grid moment: a tilted grid of feature tiles on bone, which the camera dollies across.
  - Tiles: Gold price (AED 482.65/g, "Rechecked every 10s", with a sparkline), Diamonds at real size, Delivery or collection, Gold bars & coins, Identity checks, Store visits, Excel/CSV upload (1–200), English · العربية.
  - Over the grid: "Live gold prices. Clear breakdowns." with a gold marker sweep.

## Outro / punchline
The tiles fly back into the orbit and the jade night returns as a bookend. On the strong beat at 22.65 s the GET GOLD wordmark lands with a yellow "getgold.ae" badge. The Arabic line "نضع سوق الذهب الإماراتي على الإنترنت." writes on in Aref Ruqaa gold, with the English underneath.

## User flow worth showing
1. Compare: the cards land, the cursor sorts by "Lowest final total", the winner rises and the breakdown is magnified.
2. Size: match the ring on screen, the size resolves to EU 54, then "Save as my size".
3. Request: describe the piece, send it to verified stores, and offers come back.

## Tone
- Preset: `app-store`, borrowing `cinematic` for the intro and outro.
- Creative direction: "Odoo 20 feature-launch film, made by a motion designer showing off: every transition is a move, every screen is real."
- Interpretation: the pacing is fast and the cuts land on the beat. The UI moments hold long enough to read the numbers. The craft shows in the camera moves, depth, stickers and lens, never in flashing text. The tone is clean, warm and confident, with no jokes at the product's expense.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24.5 s
This sits above the 18–22 s sweet spot on purpose. A feature-demo film needs the hook, three demonstrated flows, the breadth grid and the end card, and the flows are the strongest material. It stays inside the 25 s limit.

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Accent: gold #E9BD50 (stickers, starburst, highlights); deep gold #D69B2D / #B97820

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer under a warm bed. The edit is cut to the music.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.8 BPM, warm and business-like; its strong cues fall at every scene change we need).
- Music treatment: start at 0, around 0.34 volume, quick fade-in over 0.3 s, fade out across the last 0.9 s under the end card. Let the logo bell ring over the fade.
- Music cue guidance:
  - Preset read from `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, tempo 114.84.
  - Strong cues to lock: **1.60 s** (logo slam), **17.91 s** (feature grid), **22.65 s** (end-card wordmark).
  - Scene changes sit on the strong cues 3.70, 8.96 and 12.65.
  - Beat-grid windows:
    - Compare cards 5.28 / 5.80 / 6.34 / 6.86. The cards hold together afterwards, so this is not text read one per beat.
    - Click 7.38, sticker 7.91.
    - Size count 10.54–11.06, save click 11.60.
    - Request chips 13.70 / 14.22 / 14.76, send click 15.81.
    - Offers 16.34 / 16.86 / 17.39. They hold together; each one gets a full beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Bass makes the jade glow and gold dust breathe in the cosmic scenes, and the gradient light behind the floating windows swells. Treble adds a soft gold glow on the wordmark. No bars, no waveform, no strobing.
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched. Soft whoosh-like card slides on the whips, small drops for cards landing, real mouse clicks for the cursor, randomised key ticks for typing, a soft thud when a sticker slaps on, and the bell on the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: logo slam, whip transitions, card arrivals, the sort click, the lens, the sticker slaps, typing, the send click, offers arriving, the grid wave, the end-card bell.
- Restraint rule: no SFX on every grid tile (accent the first and last only). No bright, high-risk sounds repeated. The music never above 0.4.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Cosmic hook — 0.00–3.70 s (3.7 s)
Deep jade starfield with gold dust. A 3D-tilted ring of 16 flat gold icons orbits; the camera pushes in. The handwritten "Meet" writes on in Caveat (0.3–1.2). At 1.3 the orbit accelerates and collapses inward. At 1.60 the 3D gold ring spins in and the GET GOLD wordmark slams in with the UAE stripe. The yellow starburst "UAE" pops at 1.85. At 1.75–3.6 "Bringing the UAE gold market online." holds.
Sequential/interaction: the icons collapse into the ring; the wordmark is built from stripe, letters and starburst.
Audio intent: a build into the first downbeat, then a confident landing.
Audio-coupled idea: bell plus soft impact on the 1.60 slam; a small pop on the starburst.
Music: warm, upbeat.
Transition mood: whip pan right (dramatic) → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — GetGold Compare — 3.70–8.96 s (5.26 s)

4.75: the camera dives through the icon into a tilted floating browser window over a jade-to-gold gradient. The site header shows "24K gold · market reference · AED 482.65/g".
5.28–6.86: four store cards land one per beat: Store A · Dubai, Store B · Sharjah, Store C · Dubai, Store D · Abu Dhabi. Each shows 22K · 10g · AED 444.04/g, an "Estimated final total" and a Value Score.
