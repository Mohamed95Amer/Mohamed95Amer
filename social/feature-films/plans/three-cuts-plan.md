# Brag Plan: Get Gold, three cuts (both sides · customers · vendors)

Follow-up to `brag-output-2026-10-28-113924` (the 24.5 s Odoo-style feature film). The user asked for:
- 30–40 s cuts;
- the website **concept** made clear, not only the features;
- three audiences: customers and vendors together, customers only, and vendors only.

## What is this app?
Get Gold is a marketplace of verified UAE jewellers.
- Buyers see live gold prices, compare stores on the full breakdown, size a ring on screen, and send requests.
- The listed store confirms the final price, is paid directly and remains the seller of record.
- Founding stores list free for life.

## The angle
Keep the motion language that already landed: cosmic orbit, chapter cards with doodles, tilted real UI, yellow handwritten stickers, the magnifier, the cursor, whips, and the gold-calligraphy end card. Then add the two things a cold viewer is missing:
1. **What Get Gold is.** A hub diagram: verified jewellers in every emirate on one side, buyers on the other, and Get Gold with the live gold price in the middle.
2. **How it works.** The real steps from the site, drawn as a track that a gold token travels along. The both-sides cut uses two swimlanes, so you can see where the buyer and the store hand off to each other.

Each cut speaks to one audience: its words, its steps, its features and its call to action.

## Hook (all cuts, 0–3.7 s)
The icon orbit collapses into the spinning 3D ring, and GET GOLD slams in on the first strong beat (1.6 s).
- Both and customers: "Bringing the UAE gold market online."
- Vendors: "Bring your store online." (the site's own call to jewellers)

## Outro / punchline
Every cut ends on the jade-night bookend and the gold-calligraphy Arabic line. The call to action changes by audience:
- Shoppers: getgold.ae
- Jewellers: getgold.ae/founding-stores
- The both-sides cut shows both.

## User flow worth showing
- Buyer: compare (rank by final total) → size → request → offers.
- Jeweller: capture the tray, then drafts, then review → buyer request → send offer → paid directly.
- The split request/offer scene shows the hand-off between the two sides in one shot.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store with cinematic bookends.
- Direction: the same Odoo-20 feature-film grammar, now with an explainer spine (what it is, then how it works, then features, then the call to action).
- Interpretation: a little more breathing room on the concept scenes, where every line is a definition, and the same punchy cuts on the features.

## Audio direction
- Role: rhythmic bed with motion-matched SFX. Scene changes sit on beats, and the strong cues are locked at the hook, at one mid-film payoff and at the end-card wordmark.
- Music:
  - A and B use vol-11, preset cues at `assets/music/cues/…vol-11….json`.
  - C uses vol-9, preset cues at `…vol-9….json`. It has the same 114.84 BPM lattice, so scenes stay on the beat.
  - Volume around 0.34, with a fade-in and a fade over the last 0.9 s.
- Music cue guidance:
  - A locks 1.60, 19.49 and 35.81.
  - B locks 1.60, 28.44 and 32.12.
  - C locks 1.59, 28.96 and 30.01.
  - Beat-grid windows: hub nodes, step nodes, card landings, detection boxes and tiles all land on consecutive beats; text lines hold after the set lands.
- Audio-reactive: subtle. Bass breathes the jade and studio glow, and treble adds glow to the wordmarks.
- SFX:
  - Clicks for the cursor, keys for typing, drops for nodes and cards.
  - A shuffle for the flying request and offer, a soft impact for stickers, a glass sparkle for ticks, and the bell on the wordmarks.
- Restraint: score only the first and last of a dense sequence, and keep the music under 0.4.
