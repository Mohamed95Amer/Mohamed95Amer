# Get Gold feature films: four videos

Landscape films (16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps) in the style of Odoo's "Meet Odoo 20's best features":
- a cosmic orbit intro;
- chapter cards with line-drawn doodles;
- the real Get Gold screens in tilted 3D windows;
- yellow handwritten stickers, a magnifier, a simulated cursor and typing;
- whip transitions;
- a gold-calligraphy Arabic end card.

The three 36–40 s cuts open by explaining what Get Gold is and how it works, then show the features. Each video has a .jpg poster, also baked in as the first frame. Captions are in share-copy.txt.

**About the content**
- **Copy:** on-screen wording comes from getgold.ae and the app's own labels.
- **Prices:** compare totals use the app's pricing formula at the 24K reference rate of AED 482.65/g (22 Oct 2026).
- **Illustrative examples:** Store A–D, the offer amounts, the AI category suggestions and two of the three spreadsheet rows.
- **Music:** "Happy Beats / Business Moves" vol-11 (buyers, both sides, feature film) and vol-9 (jewellers), by ende.app.
- **Sound effects:** Kenney (CC0).
