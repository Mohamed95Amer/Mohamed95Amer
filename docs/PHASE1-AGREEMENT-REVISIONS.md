# Get Gold Phase 1 vendor pre agreement revisions

These proposed replacements align the supplied MOU with Phase 1. Retain its six-month free period from signature. This is a wording draft for UAE legal review, not an executed agreement or a finished Word document. All unchanged clauses remain subject to that review.

## Purpose and readiness

Add clause 1.4: This MOU records recruitment intentions only. Before accepting customer payments or fulfilling platform orders, the parties will enter into a definitive operating agreement and complete applicable licensing, privacy, payment and operational requirements. The binding clauses identified in clause 1.2 do not make the remaining commercial intentions enforceable.

Replace clause 3.5: Initial catalogue setup and product photography support are subject to an agreed scope, item count and schedule confirmed in writing before work starts. No unlimited photography service is promised.

## Reservations and price acceptance

Replace clause 4.2: An initial price and stock reservation lasts for the window displayed at checkout, normally ten minutes. The Vendor must accept it before expiry. Identity verification precedes the final reservation. On acceptance, the displayed total remains fixed until the accepted order's payment or fulfilment deadline. The initial Phase 1 deadlines are thirty minutes for bank-transfer payment and twenty-four hours for cash or card at delivery or collection. The customer is shown the applicable deadline. The Vendor must not accept an order it cannot complete within that window. An expired order cannot be revived without a new stock and price agreement.

Replace clause 4.4: The Vendor responds within the initial reservation window while accepting orders. If the Vendor cannot respond, the request expires without an obligation on the customer to pay. An hours-based response promise does not extend a ten-minute stock lock.

## Phase 1 fees

Replace clause 5.1: Get Gold charges the Vendor no listing fee, subscription fee or commission during Phase 1. In particular, the previously proposed commission on the Vendor's making charge is paused and is not deducted from, invoiced to or collected from the Vendor. Any future Vendor fee requires a definitive written agreement and advance written acceptance; this MOU does not create it.

Replace clauses 5.2–5.7 with: Get Gold may charge the customer a separately disclosed service fee on the merchandise subtotal. The Phase 1 standard rate is one percent. Each new customer receives a fifty-percent introductory discount on that fee for their first three qualifying orders, resulting in an effective rate of one-half of one percent. Delivery is excluded from the service-fee base. The checkout must show the effective rate, discount and AED amount before placement. Cancelled, rejected and expired unpaid orders do not consume an introductory order; paid orders retain their introductory-order position even if later refunded. This customer fee does not reduce the amount owed to the Vendor for its listed merchandise and delivery charges.

Add collection wording: Where the customer pays the Vendor directly, the Vendor collects the complete displayed total, including the separately identified Get Gold customer service fee, on Get Gold's behalf. It is not a commission on the Vendor's making charge and must not be treated as the Vendor's sale proceeds. Get Gold supplies a completed-order statement; the Vendor reconciles and remits undisputed collected service fees within the period completed in Schedule 1. No remittance is due before Get Gold is incorporated and activates a written settlement process.

## Delivery and direct payment

Retain the choice of the Vendor's own insured staff or a third-party courier appointed and insured by the Vendor. The Vendor receives customer payments, including the disclosed delivery fee, and pays its courier directly. Where the courier collects money, it does so under its agreement with the Vendor and remits directly to the Vendor, never to Get Gold.

Add to clause 6.2: Delivery is charged once per order. Store collection has no delivery fee. The Dubai cap remains AED 60; deliveries outside Dubai require a disclosed, separately agreed arrangement before order placement.

Add clause 6.6: Available methods are cash, card through the Vendor's terminal, an operational approved online payment method settling to the Vendor, and bank transfer where enabled. A method is offered only if the Vendor can actually support it for the selected fulfilment method. Online capability is not promised until implemented and tested.

Add clause 6.7: For a bank transfer, the customer first waits for the Vendor's stock acceptance, then uses the bank details captured for that order and supplies a transaction reference and receipt privately. The Vendor must verify the exact cleared amount in its own bank account. A screenshot or uploaded receipt alone does not confirm payment or authorise fulfilment. Only the Vendor's confirmation of received funds advances the order. Bank details changed later do not silently replace an existing order's instructions.

Add clause 6.8: If a transfer is delayed, short, duplicated, disputed or received after the deadline, the Vendor reconciles it with the customer and arranges any required refund. The customer must not be asked to pay twice. An expired stock hold is not automatically restored by a late receipt. Refund responsibilities, response deadlines and failed-delivery handling must be defined in the definitive agreement without limiting mandatory consumer rights.

## Identity and information

Add clause 7.4: Identity verification starts at checkout, after the customer has entered the required order details. Verification, transaction receipts and customer information are handled under documented privacy notices and access/retention rules. The Vendor remains responsible for its applicable customer due-diligence duties; the platform's identity result does not replace them. No biometric or identity-data policy is represented as legally approved by this MOU.

Replace clause 10.1: Get Gold retains rights in its software, brand, platform design and photography it creates, subject to agreed licences. This clause does not confer ownership of individuals' personal data or remove applicable privacy rights. Personal-data responsibilities and permitted uses must be specified separately before live operations.

## Matters for the definitive agreement

Counsel should reconcile personal signing and later company substitution in clause 12; the non-binding vendor indemnity in clause 7.3; binding non-circumvention scope; support and refund deadlines; transit insurance exclusions; tax invoicing; and the relationship between the fixed MOU expiry and each vendor's six-month Free Period. Add accepted payment methods and bank-account verification steps to vendor onboarding. Do not collect account passwords or OTPs.
